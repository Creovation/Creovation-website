About us

Built to connect people, not just accounts.

Creovation Media is an influencer marketing agency focused on one thing: building genuine partnerships between creators and brands. We exist because we saw both sides struggling — creators with engaged audiences who couldn't get consistent sponsorships, and brands spending money on campaigns that felt forced and disconnected.

We started this agency to fix that. Our approach is hands-on and relationship-first. We don't blast out generic pitches — we research, we match carefully, and we handle every stage of the deal so neither side has to stress about the back-and-forth.

For creators, that means showing up as a real partner who negotiates on your behalf and protects your interests. For brands, it means bringing you creators whose audiences actually care — not just large follower counts with low engagement.

We earn a commission on every successful deal, so our incentives are always aligned with yours. When you win, we win.

We're still growing, and we're proud of that. Every partnership we build is done with care, because our reputation depends on the quality of each one.

How it works

Simple by design. Thorough in practice.

Here's exactly what happens when you work with us — from the first conversation to the final payment.

1

We research and vet

We identify creators with strong, genuine engagement — real audiences, consistent posting, and content that brands feel comfortable associating with. Quality over follower count, always.

2

We make the right match

We connect each creator with brands that genuinely fit their audience. This isn't spray-and-pray — we pitch specific creators to specific brands because the alignment makes sense.

3

We negotiate on your behalf

We handle all rate discussions, deliverable scopes, timelines, and usage rights. Creators don't have to undersell themselves, and brands don't overpay for the wrong thing.

4

We protect both sides with contracts

Every deal is backed by written agreements — no ambiguity, no bypassing the process. Clear expectations protect creators, brands, and the working relationship for the long term.

5

We manage the campaign

We stay involved throughout — coordinating deadlines, reviewing deliverables, and making sure the campaign runs smoothly on both ends.

6

We take a commission when you succeed

We earn a percentage of the deal — typically 15–25% — only when the partnership closes. That's it. No retainers upfront, no hidden fees. Our success depends entirely on yours.
